About This Website

This website, Switzerland Food Journey, is created to explore the rich culinary traditions of Switzerland. It showcases some of the most iconic Swiss dishes along with their history and cultural significance.

The goal of this project is to give users a visual and informative experience about Switzerland’s famous foods while also practicing web design using HTML, CSS, and interactive elements.
